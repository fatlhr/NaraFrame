# Story

Give the character a goal and show what gets in the way. End the first shot on an action that makes the next shot necessary. Carry the pose, prop, position, and direction of travel across the cut. Let the next shot show the consequence before adding another event.

Use original pose variants with a consistent silhouette. Keep the prop visible through the change and animate it with the character when contact matters. Use brief holds for a decision, a catch, or a reaction. Check the frames on both sides of every cut at phone size with the audio muted.

Pair `note-gust` with `note-catch` for a moving prop, or `puddle-stop` with `puddle-cross` for an obstacle that changes state. Use the same title across both shots. For a new brief, author poses and props around its own cause and consequence.
