# Current Issues

Explain a current event with factual, restrained, high-contrast editorial motion graphics optimized for vertical reading.

- Lead with the immediate event, then add only the historical context needed to understand it.
- Separate confirmed facts from estimates or developing information.
- Prefer short Indonesian sentences, strong hierarchy, and one visual idea per scene.
- Use warm paper surfaces, near-black backgrounds, and orange-red accents sparingly.
- Avoid sensational imagery, decorative motion, and claims that cannot be sourced.
