# Stickman Ink

Draw light paper and fine ink silhouettes. Frame the body and prop together so weight shift, grip and release explain what changed. Keep the feet planted and establish hand contact before the prop moves. Hold the pose long enough to read the reaction. Move closer when the contact needs detail.

For a figure that bends, use the jointed ink rig from `ink-heave` and `ink-cheer`. Lead with the face swap, then the weight shift, then the limb: anticipation the other way, a move past the mark, a settle. Keep hands on what they hold through the move.
