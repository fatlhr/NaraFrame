# Motion Comic

Tell one short story in three panels. Establish the character and what they want in the first panel. Change the situation in the second and show a readable reaction or outcome in the third. Reveal panels in reading order and move the visual focus with them. Keep the earlier panels visible for context.

Use original PNG artwork with a deliberate crop for each panel. Keep speech and sound-effect text editable. Make each pose and prop readable with the audio muted, and hold dialogue long enough to read without covering the action.

Use `cookie-decoy` for a setup, theft and reaction, or `squeaky-sneaker` for a quiet setup, interruption and visual resolution. For another story, draw poses and choose crops that show its own cause and effect.
