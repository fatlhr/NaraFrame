# Contrast

A deliberate inversion of the current-issues profile, used to prove that a
project's look follows its profile and not its scenes. Light paper ground,
dark ink type, teal accent, hairline rules instead of soft shadows, and
quicker, smaller motion.

- Keep the same editorial restraint: one idea per scene, short Indonesian
  sentences, confirmed facts separated from estimates.
- Set type in lighter weights than the current-issues profile and let the
  rules and spacing carry the hierarchy instead of weight alone.
- Draw panels with a visible stroke and a small radius; reserve fills for
  the few places where a block of colour means something.
- Move less: shorter reveals, smaller emphasis, a gentler camera. Motion
  here confirms a change rather than announcing it.
- The accent is a single teal. Use it for one mark per scene at most.
