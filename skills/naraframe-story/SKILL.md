# Story

Use the brief and the character workflow in the sibling `naraframe/characters.md`. Select `story@0.1.0` and the matching catalog at `library/characters/story/catalog.json` inside the engine.

Show a goal, obstacle, action, and consequence. Carry the character pose and prop state across each cut. Pair `note-gust` with `note-catch`, or `puddle-stop` with `puddle-cross`, all at `0.1.0`. Use a shared title within the pair and describe the handoff in the shot notes. For another brief, draw original poses and review the frames on either side of the cut.
