# Story

Use the brief and the character workflow in the sibling `naraframe/characters.md`. Select `story@0.2.0` and the matching catalog at `library/characters/story/catalog.json` inside the engine. An existing episode keeps the version in its profile lock.

Show a goal, obstacle, action, and consequence. Carry the character pose and prop state across each cut. Pair `note-gust` with `note-catch`, or `puddle-stop` with `puddle-cross`, all at `0.1.0`. For a nearly still story told by a screen or a light, pair `phone-glow` with `porch-light` at `0.2.0`, or build shots from their faceless rig with the poses in *Story rig* in the sibling `naraframe/characters.md`. Use a shared title within the pair and describe the handoff in the shot notes. For another brief, draw original poses and review the frames on either side of the cut. For breath, overshoot, light changes and pose changes at a cut, follow *Nearly still characters* in the sibling `naraframe/authoring.md`.
