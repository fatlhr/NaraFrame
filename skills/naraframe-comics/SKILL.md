# Motion Comic

Use the brief and the character workflow in the sibling `naraframe/characters.md`. Select `motion-comic@0.1.0` and the matching catalog at `library/characters/motion-comic/catalog.json` inside the engine.

Reveal three panels in reading order. Move focus to the active panel and hold the dialogue through its reaction. Keep dialogue and sound effects editable, and choose a manual crop that leaves the important pose and prop in view. Use `cookie-decoy@0.1.0` or `squeaky-sneaker@0.1.0` for the matching story. For another story, draw original poses that still make sense with the audio muted.
