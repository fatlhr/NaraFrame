# Mechanic’s Bench

Use the brief and the character workflow in the sibling `naraframe/characters.md`. For a new episode, select `mechanics-bench@0.1.0`. For an existing episode, follow its profile lock. The shared catalog lives at `library/characters/meja-mekanik/catalog.json` and retains its existing pattern IDs and versions.

Keep a fixed diagonal projection and show which part drives the next. Choose `mechanik-jam`, `mechanik-linkage`, or `mechanik-flow` at `0.1.0` when the action fits. For a different mechanism, stage original parts around a visible pivot, rail, or contact point.
